Let me think about the turn-type taxonomy. The plan calls for five types: human prompt, assistant prose, tool loop, thinking-only, and harness/system. The key design constraint is that collapsed "harness" and "thinking" turns must still show their identity (type chip + request # + model + timing) even when the body is hidden. The rail colour does most of the differentiation work at a glance... For the left-rail colour mapping: blue for human (consistent with the existing blue left border on .msg--user .msg__prose and .you), green for assistant (consistent with the green right border on assistant prose and .msg--assistant .msg__role), amber for tool (consistent with .step--tool .step__kind and .step__tool-name), blue-border for thinking (a softer blue variant to distinguish from user), muted border-color for harness (demoted, not invisible).
