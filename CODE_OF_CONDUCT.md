# Code of conduct

mike follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, criticise work and not people, and
accept that a maintainer may decline a change.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** form on
[temet-ai/mike](https://github.com/temet-ai/mike/security/advisories/new), marked as a
conduct report. Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled in confidence.
